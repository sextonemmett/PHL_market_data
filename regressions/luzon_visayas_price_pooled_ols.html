Both tables use pooled Luzon and Visayas regressions with interactions by luz_vis_link_congestion.

The no-congestion columns report baseline coefficients from the pooled model when luz_vis_link_congestion = 0.

The with-congestion columns report linear combinations of baseline and interaction terms when luz_vis_link_congestion = 1.

MKT_REQT is treated as demand in MW, so demand coefficients are reported for a +100 MW change rather than a per-1-MW change.

For Luzon-price columns, “own” refers to Luzon and “other” refers to Visayas. For Visayas-price columns, “own” refers to Visayas and “other” refers to Luzon.

Significance stars: * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
