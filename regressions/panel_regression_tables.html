Panel Regression Tables

These regressions use statsmodels OLS with HC1 robust standard errors and no 5-minute slot fixed effects. Specifications 1 through 5 report the same congestion design under four calendar fixed-effect options: none, month, week, and day. Specification 6 instead reports separate day-FE regressions for Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao focal-island samples.
